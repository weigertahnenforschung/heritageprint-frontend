How to Get Your GEDCOM File

Go to Tree Settings

Open your family tree on Ancestry, click your tree name in the top left corner, and select Tree Settings.

2

Export Tree

On the right side of the settings page, look for the banner and click Export Tree.

3

Download File

Wait a few seconds for the file to generate, then click the green Download GEDCOM button.
